The Four-Stroke Engine

ME 2410 · Lecture 7
  1. Intake: piston moves down; intake valve open; fuel–air mixture drawn in
  2. Compression: piston moves up; both valves closed
  3. Power: spark ignites the mixture; expanding gas drives the piston down
  4. Exhaust: piston moves up; exhaust valve open; burnt gas pushed out
One cycle = 4 strokes = 2 crankshaft turns (720°)
Camshaft turns at ½ crankshaft speed
