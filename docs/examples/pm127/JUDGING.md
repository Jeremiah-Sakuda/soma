## PM128 calibration correction — November1

The user challenged the empirical-impact requirement. Rechecking the verbatim participant guide confirms that Impact asks about potential, who benefits, significance, likelihood of use and problem-statement fit—not demonstrated learning gains, adoption or measured recurring time saved. The panel above over-weighted absent empirical outcomes and therefore applied a stricter impact standard than the supplied hackathon rubric. Retain these scorecards as historical subjective feedback, not a properly calibrated estimate of official scoring. No replacement score has been invented. The appropriate assessment can credit credible beneficiary value and a working demonstration while withholding unsupported claims of measured results.
