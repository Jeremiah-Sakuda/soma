## The experience to aim for

**“I point at something I don't understand. Soma makes it explorable beside my work. I change it, and the explanation follows my experiment.”**

The reference's strongest visible qualities are proximity to the original work, a small amount of explanation, clear custom diagrams and easy movement between a question and its visual answer. Sampled frames show code, a technical mechanism and hardware documentation. These observations do not establish its capture architecture, model latency or reliability.

Soma can add a distinctive consequence: direct manipulation with independently computed results for supported graph search, followed by an explanation that preserves that changed experiment. A visually elaborate illustration alone is insufficient. The demonstration needs a relationship the viewer can understand, change and question.

Our largest gap is continuity: the most expressive engine visual is a recorded local prototype, while the canonical product has the full generation/manipulation/revision/save loop. A polished edit cannot substitute for closing usability gaps in that loop. Keep the engine's status explicit while improving the same interaction grammar across both surfaces.

## Today's work, in priority order

Time boxes are planning estimates, not implementation or latency promises. First complete the highest-priority working slice; avoid multiple concurrent redesigns. If time runs short, shorten the montage before cutting correctness or rehearsal.

### 1. Establish one continuous real journey — 45 minutes

Use an original, shareable Intro AI graph worksheet as the primary source. Start in the user's normal slide/PDF app; invoke Soma, point to the relevant relationship and ask one specific question. Rehearse the current native path before changing it. Record the starting revision, viewport, exact input, selected region, actual waits and failures.

Use the successful PM126 S/M/N/T scenario as the first baseline: show the initial S→M→T route, disable M–T, show S→N→T, ask why, save and reopen. Confirm the actual generated graph matches those facts before recording narration. A weighted comparison is an alternative only if it is clearer in rehearsal; do not turn this into two full graph demonstrations.

**Deliverable:** an uncut baseline take and a short list of visible blockers. If native capture or voice fails, fix the existing path first or use the explicitly identified browser/upload fallback. Do not stage a browser interaction as working native capture.

### 2. Make the companion feel attached to the work — 90 minutes

Polish the existing compact surface rather than adding navigation. Keep the relevant source visible beside one dominant visual. Preserve the selected region in the immediate receipt, then make the resulting explanation's source connection easy to inspect. Keep one question composer and one obvious playback action; place history, settings and detailed receipts behind their existing disclosures.

At the target620×704 companion size, the active visual, its primary experiment control and question action should be reachable together without a long scroll. Keep the source app readable, preserve focus, and ensure the panel does not obscure the selected content when space permits. Inspect an actual1920×1080 recording frame as well as the companion viewport.

**Acceptance:** a viewer can identify what was selected, what changed and where to ask the next question. No competing headline, duplicate explanation or setup panel displaces the visual. Keyboard and reduced-motion alternatives remain usable.

### 3. Choreograph one unmistakable consequence — 90–120 minutes

The main graph moment is an intervention, not a tour of controls. Pause on the original route; change one connection; visibly mark that intervention; show the new route and one concise consequence. Preserve layout and object identity so the viewer sees the same system changing. Where queue motion is shown, it must follow the checked trace; never invent intermediate values for visual effect.

For the engine, use the existing synchronized90°/450° comparison: same piston position, different valve/stroke roles. Direct manipulation must remain immediate and reversible. Keep its source and recorded-prototype attribution inspectable. Improve framing, label placement and meaningful motion before adding detail or camera effects.

**Acceptance:** the causal difference reads in a silent five-second clip. No overlapping cost labels, hidden control, disconnected mechanism parts, stale caption or jump back to a default state. This is a subjective editorial check plus relevant geometry/state regression checks, not a benchmark claim.

### 4. Make narration and interruption feel like one interaction — 60–90 minutes

Rehearse first audio, between-step pauses and what happens when the user drags, asks, hides the app or changes source. Match each short spoken beat to the visible event using the existing media-driven timing. Prefer one idea per beat; avoid a paragraph spoken over an unchanging diagram. Preserve captions and manual exploration while audio prepares.

The key moment: interrupt an explanation, change the experiment, then ask about the changed state. Old audio must stop; the next explanation must address that state. Do not lower reasoning/review quality, fabricate progress, or substitute prerecorded audio while claiming live synthesis. Actual providers remain attributed accurately.

**Acceptance:** a complete take with no stale narration and no unexplained dead air. Measure immediate feedback, validated-visual availability, first audio and follow-up separately. Real generation waits may still be tens of seconds; retain the uncut recording and label shortened waits in the edit.

### 5. Reveal a substantive Muse decision — 60 minutes

Show a question, the visual action Spark selected, and why that action is revealing. The existing recorded engine investigation can demonstrate this now: exact prepared question → chosen matched-pose comparison/part inspection → visible consequence. Label it recorded. Keep the canonical follow-up as the separate live/current-state proof.

Use one brief inspectable authorship view: Spark interpreted/authored or chose; Soma rendered and checked applicable computations; Wonder supplied advisory review when it actually ran. A recorded substantive repair can support this in a few seconds if it is legible. Do not add model calls or models merely to animate a pipeline badge, or imply that Wonder certified all claims.

**Acceptance:** someone can say what the Meta model decided that the renderer did not already determine. Source, action and result agree; exact model/status attribution is available without taking over the screen.

### 6. Challenge generality, then capture the final take — 90–120 minutes

Use a compact development matrix: the primary Intro AI source; a code/documentation source; an original shareable mechanism explanation; and two fresh screenshots not used to author the recording. Keep questions natural and withhold reference answers from the generation prompts. Inspect selected-region accuracy, source facts, meaningful visual structure and useful follow-ups. Count clarification, rejection and publication separately; retain every first outcome.

This checks whether polish extends beyond one recording. It is a small development check, not evidence of universal coverage. No new checked family or large evaluation program is required. Use existing artifacts for offline visual/media regression work; bound live calls by the actual remaining allowance.

Then capture the complete primary journey on the exact candidate release. Save/reopen and a follow-up after an intervention are essential. Verify actual Mac shortcuts, microphone, hide/reopen and source changes. Test keyboard/reduced motion and the real recording viewport. Run affected tests; run broader release checks if a candidate is being released. No new learner-study prerequisite.

**Deliverable:** raw uncut take, actual timing notes, final-source screenshots, claim/footage inventory and a first three-minute edit. Accessible deployment, final binary, public source/video links and submission remain distinct authorized release steps.

## Stop rules and priority tradeoffs

- Spend today's effort on source-to-result continuity, visual hierarchy, meaningful manipulation, narration and a reliable take. New model families, fine-tuning, always-on video, broader autonomy and a new3D platform are outside this plan.
- Do not make canonical engine integration a surprise prerequisite. Reusing an existing visual in the same frame may improve continuity, but real live-generation/persistence integration is a distinct scope decision. Until delivered and verified, preserve the explicit prototype transition.
- Quality failures block the corresponding footage claim. A source error or reset experiment is more damaging than a longer, honestly shortened wait. Do not tune to a recording and claim broad reliability.
- Plan a midpoint playback review after steps1–3. If the five-second consequence is weak, improve framing/interaction before spending time on additional assets. Reserve the final90minutes for rehearsal, capture and review rather than another feature.
- No placement score, winner probability or actual learner benefit follows from a more compelling video. Demonstrate credible potential and the implemented experience.

## What “ready to record” means

1. A continuous source → visual → intervention → current-state explanation → saved reopen take works on the actual candidate environment.
2. Source interpretation, solver result and visible/narrated claims agree; no unsupported source detail is silently invented.
3. The selected source remains recognizable and the main consequence is readable at the intended recording size.
4. Pointer/keyboard actions interrupt cleanly; cancellation, stale sources and failed generation preserve a usable state.
5. Fresh inference, genuine recorded output, prepared examples and local prototype footage have correct labels. Wait reductions use the measured take; voice/model roles are accurate.
6. An unfamiliar viewer can explain who it helps, what they changed and why Muse mattered after one viewing. This is a quick communication check, not a required learner study.
