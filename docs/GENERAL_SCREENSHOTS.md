# Screenshots beyond the examples

The upload/capture path sends actual image pixels to Muse Spark. It does not call the prepared-example action or attach a hidden answer key. Spark authors source interpretation, labels, steps and composition through approved typed components. Reusing a renderer does not imply reusing lesson content.

Graphs within the nonnegative BFS/Dijkstra limits and ideal supported DC circuits can become checked experiments. Other subjects can become explanatory visuals, illustrative mechanisms or clarification. They do not become verified arbitrary scientific simulations. Interpretation and prose remain fallible.

Uploads accept PNG/JPEG up to 4 MB before bounded normalization. Dense or tiny text may need a closer capture. A useful request names what you are trying to understand while leaving the source facts visible. Native cursor focus can narrow attention, but it does not guarantee exact object recognition.

[Development evaluations](GENERAL_SCREENSHOT_VALIDATION.md) report the frozen-source methods, first failures, repeats and later supported-source checks. Small source samples do not establish general reliability, fidelity on every course or improved learning. [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) explains the checked/explanatory distinction.
