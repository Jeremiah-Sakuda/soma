Wheatstone Bridge: Worked Example

Find the current through R₅.
Ideal battery and wires; D is ground.

  1. Take V_D = 0, so V_A = 12 V.
  2. Left divider: V_B = 12 · 4/(2+4) = 8 V
  3. Right divider: V_C = 12 · 8/(4+8) = 8 V
  4. I₅ = (V_B − V_C)/R₅

Balanced: R₁/R₂ = R₃/R₄, so I₅ = 0
