## Recording order

1. Capture the graph sequence first. It carries the working source/manipulation/follow-up/persistence promise. Keep the uncut take and its actual generation/follow-up durations.
2. Capture the engine hook and one recorded investigation. Start at 90°/450°, show the difference, then capture the exact prepared question/action and local export. Keep the recorded prototype label visible.
3. Open the exported HTML and capture its question, diagram and explanation. A local file export and cloud-saved revision are different outputs; label each where shown.

5. Assemble the 3:00 cut. Mark any shortened generation wait with its actual measured time. Use the receipts/repair history as optional supporting inserts, not a second architecture tour.
