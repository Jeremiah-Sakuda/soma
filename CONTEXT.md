# Soma learning context

Soma keeps useful study activities ready as coursework and circumstances change. These terms distinguish course facts, learner observations and revisable interpretations.

## Language

**Course evidence**:
Material that supports an academic fact or teaching relationship within a particular course, including its applicable version and scope. A convincing title or sender alone does not establish authority.

**Returned-work observation**:
The student's submitted work and visible marking or instructor annotation. A transcription or inferred explanation is a separate record; lateness and unreadable marking are not evidence of conceptual weakness.

**Learner response**:
What the student actually answered or predicted on a particular activity, with its conditions, help, exposure and time. Later interpretation does not replace the original response.
_Avoid_: diagnosis, mastery score

**Learner interpretation**:
A provisional explanation of observed work or responses, supported by evidence and open to contradiction. Several explanations can remain plausible at once.
_Avoid_: proven misconception, definitive diagnosis

**Diagnostic interaction**:
An optional question or experiment chosen to distinguish plausible learner interpretations. Its result changes evidential support; it does not establish broad understanding by itself.
_Avoid_: compulsory assessment

**Prepared study activity**:
A persistent lesson, practice question or diagnostic ready for the student to open, explore or revisit. Its availability is distinct from a proposal to prepare it.

**Study recommendation**:
The supported next activity or session Soma currently suggests, given course evidence, learning observations and relevant commitments. A recommendation may change or remain valid when new evidence arrives.

**Assistance**:
Help or revealing exposure associated with a response, including explanations and guided demonstrations. Completing an activity after assistance does not become independent-understanding evidence.
