# Two independent proposals: D and E

October 26, 2026. Proposed and unselected. Created in response to the request for a more familiar NotebookLM/Google Calendar feel, **without consulting the UI/UX Pro Max skill, searches, reference files, or generated recipes for these two directions**. A/B/C and the product/master are unchanged.

Open `google-inspired.html` directly, or through the local preview. No server, downloaded fonts, build, API, or account is required. Its “View A / B / C” link opens the preserved Fieldnotes comparison.

- **D · The study notebook:** three clear panels for sources, the prepared activity, and day context. Rounded light surfaces, larger sentence-case labels, a warm lesson canvas, and a familiar blue action. Best for keeping the evidence close to the learning experience; the desktop uses more columns.
- **E · The everyday agenda:** a familiar date/agenda structure next to the ready lesson, with restrained category colors and explicit event times. Best for making the commitment’s effect immediately understandable; the calendar metaphor is more prominent. The agenda is read only and illustrative, not an editor or time-proportional calendar grid.

Both keep the same synthetic interview, focus-window arithmetic, circuit lesson, pending practice, and academic/personal commitments as A/B/C. Mobile leads with the usable lesson, followed by day/agenda context. “See what changed” remains visible when the source rail collapses. No compulsory chat, external event changes, saved learning claim, or source-access claim is introduced.

Official visual references supplied and inspected by the parent agent:

- [NotebookLM three-panel redesign](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/notebooklm-new-features-december-2024/): Sources, Chat, Studio panel organization. Soma adapts the center to a prepared learning activity, without requiring conversation.
- [Google Calendar official product page](https://workspace.google.com/intl/en/products/calendar/): familiar date toolbar, event categories, agenda/time relationships, and event details.

These are independent interpretations of familiar interaction patterns. No Google logo, screenshot, product asset, or source code is embedded. All diagrams and icons are original inline SVG; fonts are local system fonts.

Validation: `node design-system/soma/directions/capture-google.cjs` exercised both direction selectors, keyboard resistance changes (4Ω → 3A branch / 5A total), lesson and source dialogs, and Escape dismissal. Both layouts fit 390px and 375px without horizontal overflow. Reduced motion enabled. Desktop, mobile, and lesson captures were visually inspected. This is focused prototype verification, not full product/accessibility acceptance.
